# Synthetic Evidence Protocol — synthetic WTP, market size and validation that can be cited

**Problem:** before the product exists, all evidence about it is synthetic. NMT still needs these numbers to choose a segment, a Job and a strategy:
- Job budget
- willingness to pay (WTP)
- reachable count
- frequency
- cost-to-serve
- customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- adoption

**Solution:** reverse-engineer what makes *any* forecast trustworthy (weather, actuarial and reference-class forecasting) and apply it to NMT.

> **A synthetic claim may be cited as evidence when:**
> 1. every input is anchored to real data
> 2. ≥ 3 independent methods agree
> 3. the method family has a **measured track record** on historical cases with known answers
> 4. it is **registered as a forecast** and scored later
>
> It is then cited *with its grade, interval and track record*, never as an observed fact.

**Builds on:**
- the canon: segmentation §61/§63 (minimum evidence), the-algorithm Step 3 ("data first, then hypothesis") and Step 7 (the RICE Confidence hierarchy), behaviour-change forces, value as prediction error
- the chat:

## 1. What NMT requires — the synthetic targets

The canon names the minimum evidence for a segment decision (segmentation §61, §63; nmt-key-theses §5, "four dimensions"). Each becomes a synthetic target quantity:

| Q | Quantity | Canon role | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Reachable count of the segment | Is it large enough to scale? | people or firms, per geography |
| Q2 | Job frequency | Size, repeat, AMPPU | occurrences / year |
| Q3 | Job budget (money now spent on the Job across all Solutions, incl. DIY) | "Market with money"; budget for unit economics | €/occurrence, €/year → market € |
| Q4 | WTP for *our* Solution | Can we earn target margin? | € distribution per segment |
| Q5 | Cost-to-serve | Margin per unit | €/customer/period |
| Q6 | CAC | Demand at target cost | €/paying customer |
| Q7 | Adoption / conversion | "Customers buy": the validation node | % per period, adoption curve |
| Q8 | Value gap vs Consideration Set | Added value (prediction-error delta) | € and time equivalent |
| Q9 | Retention | Margin per unit holds on the flow | % per period |

Synthetic **market size** = Q1 × Q2 × Q3 (and the share capturable via Q4/Q7). Synthetic **WTP** = Q4. Synthetic **validation** = Q7 + Q8 + unit economics (Q4, Q5, Q6, Q9) holding together (§5).

## 2. Evidence grades for synthetic claims

New claim status `SYNTHETIC_ESTIMATE` with a grade S0–S4. It slots into the canon's RICE Confidence hierarchy (opinion → analytics → survey → interview → MVP → sales).

| Grade | Name | Requirements (all of them) | RICE confidence equivalent | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** | Unanchored | An LLM or expert number without real-data inputs | below opinion | **Never cited.** Brainstorm only |
| **S1** | Anchored | Every input is a real-data claim with a receipt (`PUBLIC_VOICE`, `PROXY_METRIC`, official stat, filing). The formula is shown and recomputed in code | opinion+ | Scoping, EVPI ranking. Not in strategy text |
| **S2** | Triangulated | S1, plus ≥ 3 methods from **different data families** (§4) whose 80% intervals overlap. Combined in log space with weights by precision | analytics (lite) | Monte Carlo inputs, strategy ranking |
| **S3** | Calibrated | S2, plus each method family **backtested** on ≥ 20 reference-class cases with known truth, as-of dated with no leakage. Bias-corrected; **conformal** interval with empirical coverage ≥ 75% at the nominal 80% | analytics / survey-equivalent | **Citable as evidence** in segment choice, `why_we_win`, pricing, go/no-go, with mandatory label |
| **S4** | Market-touched | S3, plus a behavioural micro-signal before build (fake-door CTR at a stated price, priced waitlist, pre-order) lands inside the S3 interval. *Not an interview, and not existing customers' data* | MVP-signal | "Supported by market signal" wording allowed for that claim only |

**Mandatory citation label** (generated by code):
`Q4 WTP, segment S2: €38–€61/month (80% conformal interval), median €47 · SYNTHETIC S3 · methods: price-survival, value-ceiling, calibrated panel · backtest n=31, coverage 81%, bias ×0.92 corrected · forecast F-0042 resolves at launch+90d`

**Forbidden at every grade:**
- the words "validated", "proven" or "customers want" without "synthetic"
- presenting a synthetic estimate as observed data
- citing S0/S1 as evidence
- quoting point estimates without an interval

## 3. The five principles of the engine

1. **Anchor.** No free parameters. Every input traces to real data (§6 catalogue). The LLM may *choose methods, map data to Jobs and write the words*, never invent numbers. ("AIs write the words, math writes the numbers": Convr.)
2. **Triangulate across data families.** Methods must fail for *different* reasons:

   | Family | Source type |
   |---|---|
   | Statistical | official statistics |
   | Supply-side | filings, competitor revenues |
   | Revealed price | pricing pages, procurement, transaction benchmarks |
   | Behavioural trace | search, reviews, downloads |
   | Simulated respondents | calibrated persona panel |

   Agreement across families is evidence. Agreement within one family is not.
3. **Calibrate on the outside view.** For each method family, backtest on a **reference class**: analogous past cases (same business type, same geography band) with known truth.
   - Learn the bias (median log ratio estimate/truth) and the spread.
   - Produce **split-conformal intervals**. These are distribution-free: the interval width comes from the empirical quantile of backtest errors, so stated coverage is real coverage.
   - The reference class is documented and fixed *before* looking at results.
4. **Attack.** A red-team agent (a different vendor, clean context; chat 288–289) receives only the estimate packet. It is told to find the input or assumption that, if wrong, moves the decision most. Independent estimator agents (chat 297: N executors) never see each other's work until aggregation, because the wisdom of crowds requires independence.
5. **Register and score.** Every S2+ claim becomes a **forecast** in a registry with a resolution rule and date (e.g. "first 90 days' conversion from paid search").
   - After build, reality scores it: interval hit/miss, log error, Brier score for probabilities.
   - Scores feed back into the calibration sets (NMT's loop, Step 10).
   - Pre-registration prevents moving the goalposts.

## 4. Method cards (≥ 3 families per quantity)

### Q3 + Q1 + Q2 → synthetic market size (Job budget)

| Method | Family | Formula | Best data (IE/UK/EU first) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-A Official top-down | Statistical | Spend category ≈ Job (consumer); inter-industry purchases (B2B) | **CSO** Household Budget Survey, earnings, business demography (PxStat API); **Eurostat** HBS, SBS (enterprises + turnover by NACE), supply-use / input-output tables (what industry X spends on service Y), ICT-usage surveys (adoption rates by firm size), HETUS time-use (frequency of household Jobs); **ONS** Living Costs & Food, Annual Business Survey |
| M-B Supply-side sum | Supply | Σ revenues of Solutions in the Consideration Set + DIY/non-consumption share | **SEC EDGAR** XBRL (public companies); **Companies House** API (filed accounts, turnover for medium/large companies; SIC counts); CRO (IE); private companies: headcount × revenue-per-employee from public peers (headcount from filings, not LinkedIn) |
| M-C Bottom-up demand | Behavioural | Q1 count × incidence × Q2 frequency × spend per occurrence | Registers/census for Q1; incidence from Eurostat ICT surveys or search share; price per occurrence from pricing pages and procurement awards |
| M-D Search demand | Behavioural | Annual Job-query volume × seeker-to-buyer ratio × price | DataForSEO / Google Keyword Planner volumes and CPC; seeker-to-buyer ratio calibrated on products with known customers |
| M-E Procurement (B2B/B2G) | Revealed price | Σ award values for the Job | **TED** (EU), **eTenders** (IE), **Contracts Finder / Find a Tender** (UK): actual contract values |

**Truth set for calibration:** Eurostat SBS / ONS ABS turnover for NACE 4-digit service classes (measured sector revenue), plus categories where public filings cover most of the market. Backtest M-A…M-E as of year T−3 against measured turnover at T.

### Q4 → synthetic WTP

| Method | Family | How | Best data |
|---|---|---|---|
| W-A Value ceiling (canon value + chat 1100) | Statistical + revealed | `WTP_max ≈ monetised (V_ours − V_best_alternative)`, where `V = P(result) × outcome − Σ six costs`. Time → € via occupation wages; money direct; effort/cognitive via time; negative emotion only as a stated range | Wages: CSO / Eurostat Structure of Earnings, ONS ASHE; outcome and P from review-mined success/failure rates (`job-miner`) |
| W-B Price survival (market-cleared prices) | Revealed price | Wayback pricing histories of ≥ 20 Consideration-Set Solutions. Price points that **survived ≥ 24 months**, and increases not followed by a price-complaint spike in reviews, are cleared prices. Fit a distribution per tier | Wayback CDX API, pricing pages, review timestamps |
| W-C Transaction benchmarks | Revealed price | Median/IQR of what buyers actually paid for analogous products | **Vendr** marketplace / Pricing Intelligence Report (B2B SaaS; check per-product n and dates); **procurement awards** (B2B/B2G); app in-app-purchase price lists; **Kickstarter** pledge-tier distributions (public Web Robots datasets) for consumer hardware |
| W-D Review price-sentiment curve | Behavioural | Logistic model: P(price complaint \| price, value class) across Solutions → price where complaints exceed threshold. A Van Westendorp built from revealed text | Reviews, forums (`job-miner` receipts) |
| W-E Calibrated synthetic panel | Simulated respondents | Personas conditioned on segment receipts. Choice-based conjoint including **existing products with known prices**. Elicit with free-text answers mapped to scales (Maier et al. 2025), not numbers. **Bias-correct:** fit the panel's implied WTP for known products against their real prices, then apply the correction to our product. **Sign/monotonicity test:** WTP must rise with value attributes; otherwise reject (Brand, Israeli & Ngwe report wrong-signed WTP) | Method calibration on **Twin-2K-500** (public, includes a pricing survey); our category's known prices from W-B/W-C |

**Truth set:** products with known prices and sales ranks or transaction medians. Check how far each method's predicted WTP lands from the real price across 20–40 known products. That gives a measured accuracy, not a hoped-for one.

### Q5 cost-to-serve

Three families:
- **component pricing:** cloud and LLM token price pages × modelled usage per Job occurrence
- **analog gross margins:** public SaaS 10-K / S-1 filings
- **support load:** review/forum complaint rate × handling-time benchmarks

### Q6 CAC

- **paid search:** `CAC = CPC / (CTR-adjusted visit→lead × lead→paid)` with ranges
- **paid social:** CPM × reach from the ad platforms' own audience estimators (Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager: the official tools, not scraping)
- **analog CAC payback** from public S-1 filings

**Calibration:** public companies' disclosed CAC/payback vs the model as of filing date.

### Q7 adoption → "synthetic validation"

| Method | Family | How |
|---|---|---|
| V-A Reference-class base rates (outside view first) | Behavioural / statistical | Success and conversion base rates of analogous launches: Kickstarter success by category, Product Hunt launches → later survival, public free-to-paid and trial-conversion benchmarks, app-store category conversion benchmarks |
| V-B Agent-based switching model (canon behaviour-change forces) | Simulation anchored on traces | Synthetic population sampled from Q1 and segment shares. Each agent has: current Solution (Consideration-Set shares), Problems rate (reviews), trigger rate (seasonality, regulation dates), awareness (Q6 reach), activators loaded (channel), fears/barriers share (reviews), habit (tenure proxies), price-pain (Q4). Switch when the predicted value delta exceeds the threshold (habit, fears, price) AND a trigger fires AND activators are loaded (chat 315: the five conditions) |
| V-C Bass diffusion with published priors | Statistical | Innovation/imitation parameters from the meta-analysis literature (e.g. Sultan, Farley & Lehmann 1990), adjusted to category analogs |
| V-D Calibrated panel (relative only) | Simulated respondents | Ranks offers/messages. It never sets the absolute conversion |

**Calibration of V-B:** fit its two threshold parameters on ≥ 20 past launches with public adoption traces (download estimates, public user counts, backer counts). Then check it on held-out launches.

### Q8 value gap and Q9 retention

- **Q8** = W-A's value delta, computed on the Job graph (`jobgraph` route value). Cross-check with the share of Problems vs Aha moments in reviews for current Solutions.
- **Q9:** analog cohorts from public filings (net revenue retention), review "cancelled/switched away" rates by Solution, and category benchmarks.

## 5. "Synthetic validation" — the precise definition

A strategy is **synthetically validated at grade S3** for segment *s* and Job *j* only if all of these hold:

1. Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q7 are ≥ S3, and Q5, Q6, Q8, Q9 are ≥ S2.
2. In a Monte Carlo over the calibrated intervals (≥ 10k runs, fixed seed):
   - P(margin per unit ≥ target) ≥ 0.6
   - P(CAC payback ≤ target months) ≥ 0.6
   - P(reachable volume supports the goal on the horizon) ≥ 0.6
3. Q8 > 0 with probability ≥ 0.8 against the best Solution in the Consideration Set. This is a positive prediction error; without it, the canon says there is no value.
4. The red team found no single input whose plausible error flips conditions 2–3. If it did, that input's EVPI is reported and becomes the next test.
5. Every number in the pack carries its citation label (§2), and all forecasts are registered.

If any condition fails, the verdict is **not validated (synthetic)**, with the failing node named, read top-down along the chain to profit.

## 8. Decision rule — "synthetic GO to build"

**GO to build** when §5 holds **and** the top remaining EVPI < the cost of the cheapest S4 market-touch test.

Otherwise:
- if the top EVPI ≥ the test cost → run the S4 test first (no interviews, no customer data; it creates new market data)
- if §5 fails → pivot. That means changing the set of risky assumptions (canon RAT), not tweaking copy.

The protocol works fully up to S3 without any real-world contact. S4 is optional, but it is the only way to say anything stronger than "synthetic".
