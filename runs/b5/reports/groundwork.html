What this means

  • Decision: NO GO to build: run the cheapest market touch first. The cheapest market touch is the T4 landing-page test (not run yet; B7): 3 headlines, EUR 49 vs EUR 149, about 200 founders or PMs.
  • The 10 riskiest guesses about the customer's Job all have status unknown. J2, J3 and S2 have some supporting posts, below the bar of 3 distinct authors in 2 customer-voice families. The other 7 have no public evidence in this harvest.
  • What the data does show: the problems 1,859 app reviewers describe, grouped into 7 merged themes (12 groups before merging), and which other options they name.
  • All precision figures are against model labels: model-labelled (moonshotai/kimi-k3.1-pro), not human-verified; precision against model labels; cross-model agreement with z-ai/glm-5.3 89.7% (kappa 0.86); human check NOT DONE.

8. Method and limits

  • Producer: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Auditor and gold labels: Kimi K3.1 Pro. Cross-model check: GLM-5.3. Embeddings: Qwen3. All through TensorX.
  • Precision on the held-out test split (against model labels): USER 75/77 (PASS); trigger 3/6 (NOT MEASURED); success_criterion 2/2 (NOT MEASURED); alternative 4/5 (NOT MEASURED); problem 44/44 (PASS); aha 0/0 (NOT MEASURED); supports 1/1 (NOT MEASURED); contradicts 0/0 (NOT MEASURED). Fewer than 20 calls means NOT MEASURED. Human label check: NOT DONE.
  • T2 failed: supports plus contradicts had fewer than 20 test calls, so no node can be supported or contradicted. The product narrowed to this Radar.
  • Pool limits: App Store reviews from 9 allow-listed invoicing apps (US, GB, IE); the two unrelated "Wave" listings are excluded; there is no Reddit collector; star ratings sit in the review text but are not analysed; only 1 of the 16 HN USER posts is in this harvest. So the radar is one customer-voice family, not a market-level picture.
  • Themes: k = 12 was fixed before clustering; amendment 8 then merged the groups by a rule fixed in advance. The rule compares whole reviews, not just the problem, so it does not sort cleanly by problem: the largest theme combines crash, fee, mobile and price groups, and one price group stays on its own. Flagged themes come mostly from one app. Year counts have no per-year denominator and are not trends.
  • Alternatives: app names are matched by regex; the generic options (by hand / manually, accountant, spreadsheet) also match passing mentions such as "I have to enter it manually", so those counts are upper bounds.
  • Voices are counted once per salted author hash. No names, emails or private data are stored.
