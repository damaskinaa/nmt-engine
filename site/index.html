How it works

From public posts to a report you can check

  1. We collect public posts from forums, Q&A sites, app reviews and issue trackers, plus competitors' pricing pages and their history.

  2. We keep only posts from people using a product. Developers building one and vendor marketing are filtered out.

  3. Every claim is checked against the original text. If a quote can't be found word for word, it's removed.

  4. A person reviews every report before it reaches you.
